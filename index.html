Privacy Policy
The short version: no account, no sign-up. Your progress stays on your phone. Ads (Google AdMob) and purchases (Google Play and Unity) are run by partners who handle some technical data. Details below.

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Who we are

Sky Kingdom is a mobile game made by tinmintstudio (“we”, “us”). This page explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have. Questions? See Contact us.

What stays on your phone

  • Game data: your progress, Gold and Gems, unlocked items, settings and daily reward state are saved in a file on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Reminders: notifications are scheduled by your phone itself. Nothing about them is sent to us, and you can turn them off any time in the game’s Settings or in your phone’s settings.
  • Purchases: what you buy (like Remove Ads) is recorded on your device and can be restored through Google Play.
  • No accounts: you do not sign in, so we do not collect your name, email or phone number through the game.

Who else handles data

The game uses a few well-known services. They have their own privacy policies, linked below.

Ads

Google AdMob

Its Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device’s advertising ID and app set ID, your IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), how you interact with the app and ads (such as app launches, taps and video views), and app performance information (such as launch time and hang rate). This is used to show and measure ads, to analyse use and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it, Google’s consent form asks for your permission first, and it contacts Google to check your consent status.

Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data from apps like this

Payments

Google Play

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We only learn that a purchase happened, so we can unlock what you bought.

Purchases & engine

Unity (game engine and purchasing library)

The game is built with Unity. When you make a purchase, Unity’s in-app purchasing library reports it to Unity: the product, price and currency, the store transaction ID, a random installation ID, your device model, Android version, language, storage size, app version and install date, and your IP address. It does not include your advertising ID or your payment details. Unity uses this to run and improve its purchasing service. The Unity engine may also send basic technical information about the device to Unity.

Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Ads and your choices

  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one to revive or to double your Gold.
  • Short ads may appear between runs. Buying Remove Ads turns these off.
  • Personalised ads: on Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact name varies by phone).
  • Consent: if you are in the EEA, UK or another region that requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads load. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy options when it is shown.

Purchases

Gems and Remove Ads are bought through Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play’s refund rules. Google handles your payment details and we never see them. Unity’s purchasing library also reports basic purchase details to Unity, as described under “Who else handles data”. If you reinstall or change phones, use Restore Purchases in Settings with the same Google account.

Children

Sky Kingdom is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

Keeping and deleting data

Your game data lives only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. We do not run accounts or servers that store your personal information, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data held by Google, such as advertising identifiers, is managed through your Google and Android settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to it. We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising partners may use ad identifiers as described above.

Because we do not keep personal information about you on our own servers, most requests are handled by the services listed above. Contact us and we will help or point you to the right place.

Security

We keep no personal data on our servers, and the services above use their own security measures. No app or network is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
